SevenMirror Android captures your phone's notifications and sends them,
end-to-end encrypted, to the browsers you choose on the device. This is the
first tagged release.

## 主要更新

- **Notification mirroring**: notifications from the apps you select reach only
  the browsers you have picked as recipients. Everything is encrypted per
  recipient before it leaves the device, and the relay never sees plaintext.
- **Remote actions**: mark done, dismiss and free-form replies travel from the
  browser back to the phone over the same authenticated channel, with the exact
  notification revision rechecked before any side effect.
- **Real background connection**: a foreground service plus an application-level
  heartbeat keeps the connection alive across screen-off and Doze, and detects a
  half-dead socket within one cycle instead of waiting for the relay timeout.
- **Restarts recover by themselves**: the connection returns after a reboot or a
  process kill without opening the app.
- **Diagnosis that survives**: a bounded app-private transport timeline keeps the
  failure readable after logcat has rolled over.
- **Battery exemption guidance**: setup and the permission page explain the
  exemption and offer the system dialog, without making it a blocking
  requirement.

Upgrades keep your paired identity, your app selection and your recipient
choices; the signing identity is fixed, so a later release installs over this
one. The release APK is signed with the project certificate, not a debug key.
